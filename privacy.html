Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026 · Better Color Profile

Better Color Profile ("the App") is a Windows display calibration and color-profile utility. We respect your privacy and collect only the minimum data required to activate and protect your license.

1. Data We Collect

  • License & Verification Data: When you activate a license, the App sends your license key or registered Whop email, a one-way hardware identifier (HWID) hash, and your Windows machine name to our licensing server (bettercolorprofile.vercel.app) to enforce single-PC licensing and prevent unauthorized sharing.
  • Foreground Process Detection (Local Only): When Auto-Profile is enabled, the App checks the active foreground window process name locally on your PC to switch your monitor color profile automatically. Process names and window titles are never uploaded or logged externally.
  • Optional Feedback: If you submit the in-app feedback form, your message and optional contact email are sent to our support team.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • No screen capture, screenshots, or keylogging.
  • No game memory reading, injection, or telemetry tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of user data with third-party advertisers.

3. Payments & Third-Party Services

Purchases and subscriptions are processed securely by Whop. We do not store credit card or payment details on our servers.

4. Data Retention & Contact

License records are retained while your license is active. To request deletion or support, contact us via our Official Discord or at alroufa21@gmail.com.
